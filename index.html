Building Techniques

Simple explanations of important construction stages. Technical specifications should always be verified by the responsible engineer.

01 — Foundation

Site investigation, setting out, excavation, footing reinforcement, concrete and foundation-related works.

02 — RCC Frame

Reinforcement, shuttering and concreting for columns, beams and slabs according to structural design.

03 — Masonry

Wall construction with appropriate units, mortar, alignment, openings and curing practices.

04 — Waterproofing

Waterproofing treatment for roofs, wet areas, basements and other moisture-prone locations.

05 — Services

Coordinated electrical, plumbing, drainage and other building services before final finishes.

06 — Finishing

Plaster, flooring, painting, fixtures and final quality checks before handover.
